Our story

Sephann Enterprises is a fashion design giant based in Nairobi, Kenya.

We believe in offering you the absolute best when it comes to fashion and dressing. We strictly observe the agreed terms and conditions as well as the required and discussed fashion parameters.

We are always appraised on the new and latest fashion design trends, to keep you up-to-date with the constantly changing world of fashion design.

We have a team of experienced, energetic and creative fashion design experts whose core objective is to serve you.

Our vision

To be a leading fashion house offering long lasting and reliable fashion solutions to our local and international clients.

Our mission

To provide high quality, secure and sustainable fashion design services to our clients.

Our belief

We believe fashion is a crucial aspect of every evolving society and generation. For this reason we have laid down strong infrastructures to enable our organisation deliver the absolute best even in the future to come.

Core objective: to diligently serve all our clients and deliver creative, trendy and great looking attires that can stand the test of time and fashion.

Our specialty

Fashion design is a vast field and we focus our efforts, creativity and talent on key areas: school and corporate uniforms, staff uniforms, special event attire, home décor like curtains and sheers, and professional embroidery services.

Our approach

We first listen to your idea and carefully note down your requirements, needs, preferences and inspiration. We then provide available options for you to review and engage in a deep brainstorming session. We collect the measurements and come up with design ideas for your materials, and work on them to completion.
