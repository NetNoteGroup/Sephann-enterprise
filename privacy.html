Privacy & Cookies

How we look after your information

Information you send us

When you send an enquiry or a review, it is sent to us by email or WhatsApp. This website does not store it. We use your details only to reply to you and, for reviews you allow us to publish, to show them on this website.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small files saved on your device by a website. They help a site remember your choices and understand how it is used.

How we use cookies

Your choice: we save whether you accepted or declined cookies, so we do not ask you on every page.

Analytics (only if you accept): if we add a visitor statistics tool, it is switched on only after you accept. It helps us see which pages are popular so we can improve the website.

Your choices

You can decline cookies and still use the whole website. You can also clear cookies and site data in your browser settings at any time, and you will be asked again.
